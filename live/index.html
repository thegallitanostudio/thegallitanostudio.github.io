How it runs, start to finish.

Same concepts as my 1:1 work, applied to a team. Every step ends with something each leader actually does.

Before we meetYour rules first

We cover your strict dos and don’ts in advance: brand, comms and legal guidelines, what’s off limits, disclosure, and your AI and data policy. Nothing in the room goes past them.

Each leaderNothing yet. The guardrails are set with you and your team before anyone logs in.
Before we meetKnow the room

A working session with you or your leadership development lead: the event, the goals, who’s in the room and what this team needs to be known for, together.

Each leaderA short set of reflection questions, so they walk in with something to say.
In the roomThe why

I present to the room. Who is looking your leaders up, what they find today, and why this is part of the job. Your company is investing in YOU.

Each leaderLooks themselves up, live. (This is where the room gets quiet.)
In the roomTheir Known For

Each leader names their leadership traits, with real examples, and how their piece connects to what the team is building.

Each leaderLeaves with 3 leadership traits and one line that sums them up.
In the roomLinkedIn, hands on

Workshop time. Every leader updates their profile in the room while I walk the floor to help, guide and review.

Each leaderTakes action on their own profile before the session ends.
In the roomYour first post

I coach them on how to post something, even if they have no idea what to post. Prompts, examples and a draft by the end.

Each leaderDrafts (or publishes) a first post, reviewed by me.
In the roomTheir Known For ExecutiveOS

We build each leader’s ExecutiveOS, so the AI tools your company already approves help with comms and workflows and sound like them.

Each leaderSets up their ExecutiveOS inside your approved tools.
AlongsidePrivate 1:1s

Every leader gets their own 30 to 60 minutes with me. What’s said there stays there.

Each leaderGets time on the thing they didn’t want to ask in front of peers.
AfterReadout and follow-up

You get a readout of themes, never names, plus next steps. I can stay on for monthly support if the team wants it.

Each leaderKnows exactly what to do next, and who to ask.
