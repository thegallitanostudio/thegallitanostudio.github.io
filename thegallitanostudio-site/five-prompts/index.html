1

ChatGPT

Is it current? Checks for: Misread
Paste this

Who is [Your Full Name], [Your Title] at [Your Company]? Tell me what you found and where you found it.

If it asks which [Your Name] you mean, that's your first answer.
A good answer sounds like Your current title, the right person, and sources you'd point to yourself. “[Your Name] is Chief Operating Officer at [Company]. Sources: [yourname].com and LinkedIn.”
A weak answer sounds like An old title, someone else who shares your name, or “I couldn't find much.” “[Your Name] is a Director at [Former Company], per a profile last updated in [year].”

Claude

Is it specific? Checks for: Interchangeable
Paste this

What is [Your Full Name] known for as a leader? Give me three specifics, and where each one comes from.

Read the three out loud. Would you say them about yourself?
A good answer sounds like Three things you'd actually say, each tied to something real you did. “Known for turning around struggling regions by starting with the people doing the work.”
A weak answer sounds like Lines that could describe any VP in the building. “A seasoned executive with a proven track record of driving growth.”

Perplexity

Would a stranger trust it? Checks for: Invisible
Paste this

I'm meeting [Your Full Name] next week about [a board seat, a new role, an investment]. What should I know about them before we talk?

Perplexity lists its sources. Write down every one. That's what AI is reading about you.
A good answer sounds like How you lead and what you've done, from sources that are yours. “Colleagues count on [Your Name] for calm under pressure. They write about building teams that stay.”
A weak answer sounds like A few job titles from a directory site, or an old press release. “Limited information is available. [Your Name] appears in a [year] company announcement.”

Gemini

Do you show up? Checks for: Outshouted
Paste this

Who are the most respected [your field] leaders in [your city or industry]? Why is each one on the list?

Try two or three ways of naming your field. Notice who keeps showing up.
A good answer sounds like You're on the list, for the reason you'd want to be. “[Your Name], for [the thing you want to be known for].”
A weak answer sounds like Your peers are on it. You aren't. “Leaders often cited include [Peer], [Peer] and [Peer].”

Google's AI results

Are your words out there? Checks for: your own words
Paste this

Search “[Your Full Name] [Your Company]” and read the AI Overview. Then ask AI Mode: What has [Your Full Name] said publicly about [your topic]?

No AI Overview at all? That tells you something too.
A good answer sounds like It quotes you: a talk, an article, your own website. “In a [year] keynote, [Your Name] said [something you'd happily say again].”
A weak answer sounds like It quotes a company press release, or nothing you actually said. “[Company] announced that [Your Name] will lead [division].”
