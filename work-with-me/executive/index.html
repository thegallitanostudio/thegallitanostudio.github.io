Who is it for?

People who already have the career: C-suite leaders, SVPs and VPs, firm partners and board directors. Most referred clients go straight to Known For Executive.

How long does it take?

6 to 12 months, or built around what you need.

How much of my time does it take?

About an hour a week outside our sessions: thinking, self-reflection, and the art of the possible. No book. No workbook.

What if my quarter gets busy?

We pause. Big interview in ten days? We speed up. Nothing you’ve done is lost.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes, for Known For Executive. A company can sponsor one leader through 1:1 Executive Advisory, and gets the Known For Scorecard. First Look and the Accelerator are for leaders paying personally. See For companies.

What is ExecutiveOS?

A chatbot forgets you the moment you close the tab. An ExecutiveOS doesn’t. It’s your own AI, built for you inside the tools your company already approves. It knows your story, your voice and your work. You set the direction. It does the first pass. See ExecutiveOS.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Reputation is currency. Every strategic advisory engagement is strictly guarded. Your narrative belongs to you, never my portfolio.
