Questions · Known For Executive

What people ask before they book.

Who is it for?

Executives who already have the institutional track record: C-suite officers, SVPs and EVPs, managing partners, and board directors. Most referred clients go straight to Known For Executive.

How long does it take?

A 6-month retainer. It opens with 30 days of the Accelerator: your story, your readout, your LinkedIn and your Look. Then the retainer carries it into every room that follows.

How much of my time does it take?

Known For Executive: 1 to 2 hours a month of focused strategic dialogue, depending on your needs. Known For First Look: two weeks, with sessions set around your calendar. Outside our sessions, about an hour a week of your own thinking time. No book. No workbook.

What if my quarter gets busy?

If a board interview moves up by ten days, we accelerate. If an enterprise crisis hits, we pause. Your momentum is never lost.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes. A company can sponsor one leader through 1:1 Executive Advisory, and gets the Known For Scorecard. See For companies.

What is ExecutiveOS?

A chatbot forgets you the moment you close the tab. An ExecutiveOS doesn’t. See ExecutiveOS.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Reputation is currency. Every strategic advisory engagement is strictly guarded. Your narrative belongs to you, never my portfolio.
