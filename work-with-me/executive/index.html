The architecture of the retainer

Your public footprint is the downstream outcome of your leadership thesis, not the starting point.

The engagement opens with a concentrated 30-day foundation sprint to extract and build your narrative baseline, then expands into ongoing retained advisory to carry that authority into every room that follows.

01

The strategic foundation

First 30 days

  • Narrative Diagnostic: A day-one strategic audit assessing the gap between your real enterprise impact and current market perception.
  • The Known For Architecture: Articulating your operational thesis, leadership convictions, and the specific domain you own, grounded in a comprehensive written readout.
  • Where it shows up: your collateral, written in your voice:
    • Your LinkedIn and executive narrative.
    • Your executive bio, board bio, résumé and board-ready dossier.
    • Keynote bios tailored to your talk.
    • Your banner, one-pager and deck.

02

The ongoing advisory

Months 2 to 6

  • Strategic room prep: Dedicated prep, narrative calibration, and objection anticipation for board appearances, investor meetings, and town halls.
  • The sounding board: A trusted, confidential room outside your internal org chart to stress-test private pivots, sensitive transitions, and executive decisions.
  • Elastic pacing: If a board interview moves up ten days, we accelerate. If an enterprise crisis demands your focus, we pause. Your momentum is never lost.
  • The executive readout: Measured from day one to final calibration.

03

ExecutiveOS: private intelligence

Built alongside

You cannot lead what you do not understand, but enterprise leaders need leverage, not technical theory. We build and deploy private, agentic intelligence models directly inside your company’s approved tools (Copilot, Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT).

  • Calibrated to your voice: Trained on your strategic narrative, operational priorities, and leadership cadence.
  • High-leverage workflows: Automated scenario stress-testing, objection anticipation, and first-pass executive communications running quietly in the background.
  • Time recovery: Routine work moves to your ExecutiveOS, and you review everything that goes out in your name.
  • Hands-on AI coaching: Automation and coaching for your day to day, inside the same approved tools.

The cadence

  1. 1
    Monthly strategic session

    One standing session a month, set around your calendar.

  2. 2
    Planned prep

    One 30-minute prep session a month, separate from the strategic session. A second in any month with a board, earnings or investor date.

  3. 3
    Between sessions

    Up to four short reviews a month: one short item and one round of feedback each. Replies to brief questions and reviews within two business days. Larger work is scheduled separately.

  4. 4
    Phone a friend

    Included in your fixed fee, for the moments that matter: a pep talk, a last-minute point, styling guidance. Separate from your four reviews, and it doesn’t count against planned prep. We plan the calls you know are coming, and I make room for unexpected ones when I’m available. It isn’t always-on access.

  5. 5
    Execution offloaded

    The diagnostic drafting, structural synthesis, and AI builds happen entirely on my side.

  6. 6
    Every word is yours

    If you wouldn’t say it in the boardroom, it doesn’t exist in your materials. You review, calibrate, and own every word.

I’m an executive, too. I know the Slacks, Zoom calls, and the decisions that follow you home. That shared experience shapes how I advise you. We make space for your next move through evening and weekend sessions, arranged in advance.

Questions · Known For Executive

What people ask before they book.

Who is it for?

Sitting C-suite officers, incoming executives, SVPs, managing partners, and board directors who already have the institutional track record. Most referred leaders engage directly at this level.

How long does it take?

Private Client: a 6-month retainer. Company-Sponsored: 3, 6 or 9 months. It opens with a 30-day foundation sprint to build your narrative baseline. Then the retained advisory carries it into every room that follows.

How much of my time does it take?

A monthly strategic session, plus one 30-minute prep session a month and a second in months with a board, earnings or investor date. Between sessions, up to four short reviews a month, with replies within two business days. Phone a friend for the moments that matter is included. No homework, no workbooks, no administrative overhead.

What if an unexpected crisis or board cycle hits?

The retainer is built around executive reality. If a high-stakes board interview moves up ten days, we compress timelines and prep immediately. If an operational crisis demands your attention, the engagement pauses. You never lose time or momentum.

Can my company sponsor this retainer?

Yes. Organizations can sponsor this engagement as part of executive onboarding, succession readiness, or retention, as the Company-Sponsored version, for 3, 6 or 9 months. The sponsor receives the Known For Scorecard, measuring baseline-to-endpoint growth and organizational alignment, while the executive’s private working sessions remain strictly confidential. See For companies.

What makes ExecutiveOS different from standard AI tools?

A generic chat tool starts from zero every time you open it. ExecutiveOS is a private, governed system configured to remember your strategic thesis, your operational priorities, and your authentic tone, executing first-pass communications and prep that actually sound like you. See ExecutiveOS.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Reputation is currency. Every strategic advisory engagement is strictly guarded. Your narrative belongs to you, never my portfolio.
