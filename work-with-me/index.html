Questions

What people ask before they book.

Who is this for?

Executives who already have the institutional track record: C-suite officers, SVPs and EVPs, managing partners, and board directors. You’ve done the work. Now own the narrative.

Is this a ghostwriting service?

No. Ghostwriting produces generic, polished noise. This is strategic positioning. We extract your actual philosophy, operational wins, and leadership convictions. You review and own every single word.

How much of my time does it take?

Known For Executive: 1 to 2 hours a month of focused strategic dialogue, depending on your needs, plus about an hour a week of your own thinking time. Known For First Look: two weeks, with a tailored intake, a 60-minute extraction session, the build, a 30-minute readout and final calibration sessions. No book. No workbook.

How long does it take?

  • Known For First Look 2 weeksThe strategic narrative sprint.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Reputation is currency. Every strategic advisory engagement is strictly guarded. Your narrative belongs to you, never my portfolio.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes, for Known For Executive. A company can sponsor one leader through 1:1 Executive Advisory, and gets the Known For Scorecard. First Look is for leaders paying personally. See For companies.
