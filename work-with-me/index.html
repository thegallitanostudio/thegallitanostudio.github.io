Questions

What people ask before they book.

Who is this for?

People who already have the career: C-suite leaders, SVPs and VPs, firm partners and board directors. You’ve done the work. Now claim it.

Is this LinkedIn ghostwriting?

No. We start with your story: who you are, how you lead, and where you’re taking people. Your LinkedIn is the outcome of that story, not the start of it. You read every line out loud, and if you wouldn’t say it, we change it.

How much of my time does it take?

About an hour a week outside our sessions: thinking, self-reflection, and the art of the possible. No book. No workbook.

How long does it take?

Known For First Look is the smallest way to start, and takes two weeks, with drafts and revisions. Known For Accelerator takes one month. Known For Executive runs 6 to 12 months, or is built around what you need. Go further within 60 days of First Look and all of it counts toward your next program.

Is it confidential?

Yes. I never name clients. Not who you are, not where you work, not what you tell me.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes. A company can sponsor one leader, with the Known For Scorecard: four measures, taken on day one, at the midpoint and at the end. Or bring Known For Live to a leadership team, on-site. See For companies.

What is ExecutiveOS?

It’s part of Known For Executive. I build it inside the AI tools your company already approves, so it knows your story, your voice and your work. We test it before you rely on it. You set the direction. It does the first pass.

Book a 20-minute conversation. Tell me where you’re headed, and I’ll tell you where I’d start. If someone referred you, start with the Referred form on the Book page. Book a conversation or start here if you were referred.
