What people ask before they book.

Who is this for?

People who already have the career: C-suite leaders, SVPs and VPs, firm partners and board directors. You’ve done the work. Now claim it.

Will you write my LinkedIn for me?

We write it together, and every word is yours. We start with your story: who you are, how you lead, and where you’re taking people. Your LinkedIn is the outcome of that story, not the start of it. You read every line out loud, and if you wouldn’t say it, we change it.

How much of my time does it take?

About an hour a week outside our sessions: thinking, self-reflection, and the art of the possible. No book. No workbook.

How long does it take?

Known For First Look is the smallest way to start, and takes two weeks, with drafts and revisions. Known For Executive is a 6-month retainer that opens with 30 days of the Accelerator: your story, your readout, your LinkedIn and your Look. Then the retainer carries it into every room that follows. Go further within 60 days of First Look and all of it counts toward Known For Executive.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Reputation is currency. Every strategic advisory engagement is strictly guarded. Your narrative belongs to you, never my portfolio.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes, for Known For Executive. A company can sponsor one leader through 1:1 Executive Advisory, and gets the Known For Scorecard. First Look is for leaders paying personally. See For companies.

What is ExecutiveOS?

A chatbot forgets you the moment you close the tab. An ExecutiveOS doesn’t. It’s your own AI, built for you inside the tools your company already approves. It knows your story, your voice and your work. You set the direction. It does the first pass. It’s part of Known For Executive. See ExecutiveOS for companies.
