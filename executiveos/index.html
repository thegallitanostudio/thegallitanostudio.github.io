The part nobody says out loud

If you’re approving AI roadmaps on second-hand summaries, you aren’t leading from the front. You aren’t leading it at all.

Over the last two years, executives have learned the language of AI: governance, risk, the board strategy. Behind closed doors, very few are using it themselves.

  • The expectations are unforgiving.

  • You’re supposed to have the answers to a shift nobody was trained on.

  • You don’t need another white paper. You need a confidential room where you can sit down, roll up your sleeves, break it, and build something that gives you your week back.

Where we start

Both.

  • It helps you grow. Your leaders go first on AI, in their own voice. Their teams follow the ones who use it well.

What happens if we do nothing? Is this now, or can it wait?
