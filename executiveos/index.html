Questions · ExecutiveOS

What companies ask before they book.

What is an ExecutiveOS?

A chatbot forgets you the moment you close the tab. An ExecutiveOS doesn’t. It’s one leader’s AI, built for them inside the tools your company already approves. It knows their story, their voice and their work. They set the direction. It does the first pass.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Copilot, Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT. Whichever your company has approved. There is no new software to buy from me.

Does the AI write as our leaders?

No. It does the first pass. Your leader sets the direction, reads it out loud, and changes anything they wouldn’t say. Every word is still theirs.

What can it see?

Your AI and data policies come first. An ExecutiveOS lives inside your approved tools, and it can see a leader’s files, calendar and email only as far as your policy allows.

What does the sponsor see?

Counts and averages. Never names, never quotes. Your leader’s conversations stay with me.

How many leaders are in a cohort?

We set that together. Smaller groups see the best success, because every ExecutiveOS is built one to one.

Can we start with one leader?

Yes. One leader, one ExecutiveOS. You can add leaders later.
