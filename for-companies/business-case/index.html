For your CEO and CFO

The five questions they’ll ask.

What kind of investment is this? Does it help us grow, save, or both?

Both.

It protects what you’ve already spent. You invest heavily to hire and promote senior leaders. Only 15% of board members say their organization did a very strong job preparing its first-time CEO.

It helps you grow. Investors, boards and the people you want to hire look your leaders up before they meet them. 76% of institutional investors would be likely to assign a valuation premium to a company led by a highly trusted and credible executive team.

Is this already in the budget? Does it use what we already pay for?

It fits what’s already on your calendar. Known For Live runs inside the annual kickoff, the leadership summit or the board retreat you’re already planning.

1:1 Executive Advisory sits beside the executive development you already fund.

What happens if we do nothing? Is this now, or can it wait?

It can’t wait. Waiting has a cost. Decisions about your leaders are being made fast, by people with very little attention to spare. They search a name, take what they find, and move on.

Today they find an old profile, a bio from two roles ago, and whatever else comes up. Stakeholders are searching either way. Waiting doesn’t pause that. It only decides what they find.

What are our other options? Can we do this with the people we have?

Partly. Your communications team speaks for the company. A coach works with a leader in private.

What’s left is the part people can see: each leader’s own story, in their own words, everywhere they’re looked up. That’s the part I do. Every word is theirs.

What exactly are we approving?

One engagement, agreed in writing. Known For Leadership Collective: Known For Live, an intensive alignment session with the full leadership group, then structured 1:1 advisory sessions with each executive over 30 to 90 days. Every executive leaves with a bespoke Known For Story and an aligned public profile. The bench leaves with a unified Collective Leadership Narrative, your enterprise Known For Leadership Hub and an ExecutiveOS operational cadence for every leader. For a single executive: 1:1 Executive Advisory, Known For Executive sponsored by the company.
