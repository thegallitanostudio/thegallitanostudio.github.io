Known For Live

What is Known For Live?

The session that opens Known For Leadership Collective: an intensive alignment session with the full leadership group, on-site or virtual.

Where does it fit?

Executive committee meetings, enterprise leadership summits, ELT offsites and board retreats. On-site or virtual.

How long is it?

The session runs half a day to multiple days.

Can you work with a larger group or a cohort?

Yes. I can open with a large-room session for a whole cohort or academy, then work one to one with the executives you name. The big room is for reach.

How do we choose how deep to go?

Together, once I know your team and what the session is for.

Does it have to be the whole leadership team?

No. It can be your own bench. A CHRO brings me in for their VPs. A CEO for the executive committee. Tightly curated cohorts see the best results.

Every executive’s story first, in 1:1 sessions. Then I write the team’s story from every executive’s, refine it with your executive committee, and hand it back. Every individual narrative reinforces it.
