Known For Live

What is Known For Live?

On-site, with your whole leadership team. I work with the whole room, then sit with every leader one to one.

Where does it fit?

Annual kickoffs, leadership summits and board retreats.

How long is it?

The session runs half a day to multiple days. The private 1:1s follow over ten weeks, on-site or virtual, starting with your key leaders.

Can you work with a larger group or a cohort?

Yes. I can open with a large-room session for a whole cohort or academy, then work one to one with the leaders you name. Smaller groups see the best success. The big room is for reach.

How do we choose how deep to go?

Together, once I know your team and what the session is for.

Sponsor one leader

What does sponsoring one leader cover?

Their Known For Story, their LinkedIn and their ExecutiveOS. You get the Known For Scorecard. Known For Website and Look stay with programs a leader pays for personally.

Which program is it, and how long?

Known For Executive, for 3, 6 or 12 months.

Who do you answer to?

Two people: your leader, and you. Your leader’s conversations stay with me.

ExecutiveOS

What is ExecutiveOS?

One leader’s AI, built for them inside the AI tools your company already approves. It knows their story, their voice and their work. See ExecutiveOS.

Is it part of Known For Live?

No. It’s its own work, for a cohort of your leaders or for one. It’s also part of Sponsor one leader.

Measurement and privacy

How will we know it worked?

The Known For Scorecard: four measures, taken on day one, at the midpoint and at the end, with the same questions and the same people every time.

What do we see?

Counts and averages. Never names, never quotes. No rater round with fewer than 3 people.

Do you measure followers or profile views?

No. Followers and profile views aren’t in your control, and they aren’t the point.

Is it confidential?

Yes. I never name clients. Not who you are, not where you work, not what your leaders tell me.
