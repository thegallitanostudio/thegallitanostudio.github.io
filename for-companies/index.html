How progress is measured

The Known For Scorecard

We do not track vanity metrics. Followers and profile views are not the objective.

The Known For Scorecard measures institutional alignment, narrative command, and market perception across three milestones:

  • Day one (baseline)
  • Midpoint
  • Final calibration
  1. 1

    Narrative command (Internal)

    The executive articulates their leadership thesis: who they are, how they lead, and where they are taking the organization. Evaluated on clarity, specificity, and authenticity against a standardized executive rubric.

  2. 2

    Perception alignment (External)

    A curated 360° perception audit involving 5 to 8 key stakeholders (board members, managers, peers, direct reports) measuring whether the market and internal teams hear the story the executive intends to project.

  3. 3

    Behavioral attribution

    Targeted scoring on specific executive presence shifts: how the leader commands the room, drives organizational change, and handles executive objections.

  4. 4

    Target strategic outcomes

    1 to 3 enterprise milestones agreed upon in writing on day one (for example, succession validation, board committee confidence, keynote authority).

  • Sponsors receive aggregated data, progress curves, and verified outcomes. Zero individual attribution leakage.
  • No rater round with fewer than 3 people.
