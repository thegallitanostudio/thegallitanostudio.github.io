2023 to 2026 · The arena

The real story lives off the org chart.

At Gartner, I started with CHROs and their HR leadership teams at some of the largest organizations in the country: Fortune 500 manufacturers and logistics companies, major health systems and health plans.

When a company goes through a massive shift, internal teams freeze. Everyone has a version of the story and none of them match.

  • A new CEO
  • A re-org
  • An AI pivot

CHROs brought me into their boardrooms and walked me down the hall to their CIOs because I answered the questions their internal teams couldn’t.

  • I sat right between HR and IT

  • Cut through the politics, and got two completely different sides of the house to agree on one story

  • One story the board actually believed, and one their leaders could tell with a straight face.

  • A top-ranked children’s hospital

    A new CHRO’s priorities, connected to the CIO’s: AI literacy and digital transformation.

  • A health plan

    The work started in HR. It ended with the executive vice president responsible for most of the organization.

  • A global logistics company

    It ran from the VP of HR all the way to the chief data and technology officer.

It was always about listening. Asking the question others missed, and noticing which executive everyone was quietly following.

And I recognized something. Their stories were being told for them, too. By a bio somebody else wrote. By whatever comes up when you search their name.
