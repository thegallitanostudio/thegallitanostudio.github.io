Global Rock Your Profile Leaders at LinkedIn

In March 2020, as the enterprise landscape shifted remote, I ran a session for an enterprise client on commanding digital presence. That single session scaled into 100 enterprise rooms in five months.

Selected as one of three Global Rock Your Profile Leaders, I oversaw and trained the global curriculum, reported to the CMO, and certified facilitators across the organization. We deployed sessions across 15+ industries: banking, healthcare, technology, manufacturing, and law.

Following those sessions, senior executives began reaching out privately. Their careers were moving at enterprise speed, but their public narrative was frozen years in the past. That was where the advisory took root.

2023 to 2026 · The arena

Inside the enterprise arena.

At Gartner, I partnered directly with CHROs and executive leadership teams across Fortune 500 manufacturers, global logistics giants, and premier health systems.

When an organization navigates an inflection point (a new CEO appointment, an unannounced restructuring, or an AI transformation), internal teams freeze. Everyone has a version of the story, and none of them align.

CHROs brought me into boardrooms and connected me directly with their CIOs because I sat at the intersection of workforce dynamics and technology. We cut through the political friction to build a singular, defensible narrative that boards trusted and executive leadership could deliver with conviction.

Across every engagement, the pattern held: brilliant leaders were allowing their institutional reputation to be authored by default, by an outdated bio or whatever fragmented search results surfaced online.

The Studio

The name came in 2022. The standard came after.

The foundation began in 2022 as Gallitano Creative, centered on the discipline of personal narrative. Leaders sought out private counsel before their biggest moves: VP-and-above career shifts, interview prep, résumés and salary negotiations.

In those same years, my day work moved into the C-suite: CEO succession, board presentations and enterprise AI.

The Gallitano Studio is where the two meet. Built for the altitude of the rooms I operate in today.
