About Angela Gallitano

Your story is already being told. The question is who’s telling it.

This isn’t only about knowing your story. It’s about taking control of it, so the story people find matches who you are today.

You don’t leave your past behind to do that. I didn’t. Everything that brought you here comes with you. We root in it, and then we write what comes next, in your words.

Be known for who you actually are.

My story

My family called them phases.

Vegan. Painting. Photography. Two years of high school where everyone called me Gigi. (My hair color had phases of its own.) Every six months I found something new, and I went ALL in.

It was the family joke. My brother-in-law once said, “Gigi has phases?!” and everyone laughed.

Nobody asked what I was looking for.

01 · The search

I was looking for my thing.

I’m the youngest of four, in a family of doctors, attorneys and politicians. My siblings found their thing early and stayed with it. I kept looking for mine.

Three colleges. Seven majors. Linguistics. Nutrition sciences. Cultural anthropology. Pre-med. Even Ancient Greek. Zoology won. (It has been far more useful in boardrooms than anyone warned me.)

My whole life, other people told my story. I was still looking for the part that was mine.

02 · LinkedIn

Then one of them stuck.

2020 to 2023
Selected as1 of 3

Global Rock Your Profile Leaders at LinkedIn

March 2020. The world went remote, and I was at LinkedIn. I offered one customer’s team a Rock Your Profile session on how they show up online.

One session turned into 100 in five months, on top of my quota. Customers started asking if they could buy it. Some recorded the sessions and used them to onboard their new hires.

  • 100sessions in the first five months
  • 15+industries

I built every session for the room it was in. Before each one, I worked with the company to set what their people needed to walk away with. A sales team got a different session than a room of recruiters. A leadership team got a different one than summer interns.

  • Every level

    Summer interns and new hires. Managers, directors and VPs. Law firm partners, CEOs and the C-suite.

  • Every function

    Sales, recruiting, marketing, consulting, HR and leadership teams. Job seekers, too.

  • 15+ industries

    Software, healthcare, banking, wealth management, insurance, manufacturing, logistics, legal, media and more.

Then LinkedIn nominated me as one of three Rock Your Profile leaders worldwide. From Chicago, I oversaw and trained the global program and reported to the CMO. I certified the facilitators who came after me, and I taught other account directors to run sessions of their own.

After every session, I offered a one to one. That’s where executives started finding me.

That one wasn’t a phase.

It took me a while to see why. The thing that finally stuck was helping other people tell their story.

03 · The arena

The real story lives off the org chart.

After LinkedIn came Gartner. I sat with CHROs and HR executives at some of the largest organizations in the country: Fortune 500 manufacturers and logistics companies, major health systems and health plans. Together we worked through AI strategy, CEO succession and board presentations.

It was always about listening. Asking the question others missed, and noticing which executive everyone was quietly following.

People told me the real story. (And I mean the REAL story.) I used it only to help: shaping my guidance to each situation, seeking out other perspectives, and keeping every client’s name private.

Customers tell me they’re thinking about leaving before they tell their boss. Executives call me about the conversation they can only have outside their own company.

That happens because they’ve watched what I do with what they tell me.

And I recognized something. Their stories were being told for them, too. By a bio somebody else wrote. By whatever comes up when you search their name.

04 · The name

This work existed long before it had a name.

For years, people pulled me aside. A founder explaining what their company actually does. An executive rewriting their story before a board meeting. A leader finding the words for the first time.

350+ rooms. 125+ executives one to one. The US, Italy, the UK, Hong Kong and beyond. After sessions, in hallways, on calls that ran long, inside other companies’ programs. I’m proud of that work.

Salesforce

By day, I’m a Strategic Account Director at Salesforce, working with healthcare organizations on AI transformation. I love it. I’m on the forefront of how AI is changing the way leaders work, and it’s where my AI expertise and ExecutiveOS truly come into play.

Then I noticed I’d been helping everyone else take their story back. Everyone except me.

So I gave the work a name, and put mine on it.

  • 350+rooms since 2020
  • 125+executives one to one

  • US, Italy, UK, Hong Kongand beyond

I earn trust, and then I protect it.

I never name clients. Not who you are, not where you work, not what you tell me.

I open doors when it helps. I’ve built a strong network, and when an introduction to a peer would help you, I’ll make it. Only when it makes sense, and only with your permission.

No one can write your leadership story for you. So we write it together.

Then we make sure it’s found in the right places, at the moments that matter.

Tell me where you’re headed. I’ll tell you where I’d start.
