The Gallitano Studio: new look and site update (Oct 7, 2026)

What this upload changes
  - New colors and type: sage, mahogany and purple, set in Instrument Sans.
  - New wordmark in the header and footer: the table mark is the "o" in Studio.
  - New homepage headline: "Right now you are known for your title. Be known for how you think."
  - Known For is a raspberry pill in headlines and program names, raspberry italic in sentences.
  - Duotone icons on the method steps and the "right places" row.
  - Photos: the striped-dress photo only, once on Home and once on About.
  - Audience labels: C-suite, SVPs & VPs, Firm partners, Board directors, Executive teams.
  - First Look now says "2 weeks, with drafts and revisions."
  - For companies: the Gartner press-release panel, and the McKinsey line reads cleanly.
  - The sample Look board on Work with me has its own colors.
  - New link-preview image and phone icon.

How to upload (about 5 minutes)
1. Unzip gallitano-site-update-2026-10-07.zip. You'll see a folder called "upload".
2. In GitHub, open the repository thegallitanostudio.github.io.
3. Choose Add file > Upload files.
4. Open the "upload" folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING inside it
   (the files and the folders), and drag it all onto the GitHub page.
   Dragging the folders keeps each file in the right place.
5. Scroll down and press "Commit changes".
6. Wait about a minute, then open thegallitanostudio.com and refresh.
