Questions by topic

Working with me

Who is this for?

People who already have the career: C-suite leaders, SVPs and VPs, firm partners and board directors. You’ve done the work. Now claim it.

Will you write my LinkedIn for me?

We write it together, and every word is yours. We start with your story: who you are, how you lead, and where you’re taking people. Your LinkedIn is the outcome of that story, not the start of it. You read every line out loud, and if you wouldn’t say it, we change it.

How much of my time does it take?

About an hour a week outside our sessions: thinking, self-reflection, and the art of the possible. No book. No workbook.

What if my quarter gets busy?

We pause. Big interview in ten days? We speed up. Nothing you’ve done is lost.

I don’t know my LinkedIn login. Is that a problem?

You’re in good company. Most of my clients don’t. That’s where we start.

Is it confidential?

Yes. I never name clients. Not who you are, not where you work, not what you tell me.

The programs

How long does it take?

Known For First Look is the smallest way to start, and takes two weeks, with drafts and revisions. Known For Accelerator takes one month. Known For Executive runs 6 to 12 months, or is built around what you need. Go further within 60 days of First Look and all of it counts toward your next program.

Does Known For First Look count toward a bigger program?

Yes. Go further within 60 days and all of it counts toward your next program, Known For Accelerator or Known For Executive.

When is Known For Accelerator the right fit?

When there’s a date on the calendar: an interview, a new role, a board conversation. For when you need it done, and done well, fast.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes, for Known For Executive. A company can sponsor one leader, and gets the Known For Scorecard. First Look and the Accelerator are for leaders paying personally. See For companies.

For companies

What is Known For Live?

On-site, with your whole leadership team. I work with the whole room, then sit with every leader one to one.

What does sponsoring one leader cover?

Their Known For Story, their LinkedIn and their ExecutiveOS. You get the Known For Scorecard. Known For Website and Look stay with programs a leader pays for personally.

How will we know it worked?

The Known For Scorecard: four measures, taken on day one, at the midpoint and at the end, with the same questions and the same people every time.

How do we pay?

Investment will be shared in our 20-minute conversation.

What do we send our CEO and CFO?

The business case, on one page.

ExecutiveOS

What is an ExecutiveOS?

A chatbot forgets you the moment you close the tab. An ExecutiveOS doesn’t. It’s one leader’s AI, built for them inside the tools your company already approves. It knows their story, their voice and their work. They set the direction. It does the first pass.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Copilot, Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT. Whichever your company has approved. There is no new software to buy from me.

Can we start with one leader?

Yes. One leader, one ExecutiveOS. You can add leaders later.
