Questions by topic

Working with me

Who is this for?

People who already have the career: C-suite leaders, SVPs and VPs, firm partners and board directors. You’ve done the work. Now claim it.

Will you write my LinkedIn for me?

We write it together, and every word is yours.

  • We start with your story: who you are, how you lead, and where you’re taking people. Your LinkedIn is the outcome of that story, not the start of it.

  • You read every line out loud, and if you wouldn’t say it, we change it.

How much of my time does it take?

About an hour a week outside our sessions: thinking, self-reflection, and the art of the possible. No book. No workbook.

What if my quarter gets busy?

We pause. Big interview in ten days? We speed up. Nothing you’ve done is lost.

I don’t know my LinkedIn login. Is that a problem?

You’re in good company. Most of my clients don’t. That’s where we start.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Reputation is currency. Every strategic advisory engagement is strictly guarded. Your narrative belongs to you, never my portfolio.

The programs

How long does it take?

  1. Known For First Look

    2 weeks

  2. Known For Executive

    6 months

Does Known For First Look count toward a bigger program?

Yes. Go further within 60 days and all of it counts toward Known For Executive.

What is the Accelerator?

The first 30 days of Known For Executive: your story, your readout, your LinkedIn and your Look, done and done well. Then the retainer carries it into every room that follows.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes, for Known For Executive.

  • A company can sponsor one leader through 1:1 Executive Advisory, and gets the Known For Scorecard.

  • First Look is for leaders paying personally. See For companies.

For companies

What is Known For Live?

The session that opens Known For Leadership Collective: an intensive alignment session with the full leadership group, on-site or virtual.

  • The group work comes first.

  • The structured 1:1 advisory sessions with each executive follow.

What does 1:1 Executive Advisory cover?

One executive, sponsored by the company.

  • A bespoke Known For Story
  • An aligned public profile and market positioning
  • An ExecutiveOS operational cadence
  • The Known For Scorecard

Known For Website and Look stay with programs a leader pays for personally.

How will we know it worked?

The Known For Scorecard: four measures, taken on day one, at the midpoint and at the end, with the same questions and the same people every time.

How do we pay?

Investment will be shared in our 20-minute conversation.

What do we send our CEO and CFO?

The business case, on one page.

ExecutiveOS

What is ExecutiveOS?

One leader’s AI, built for them inside the tools your company already approves. It knows their story, their voice and their work. They set the direction. It does the first pass.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Copilot, Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT. Whichever your company has approved. There is no new software to buy from me.

Can we start with one leader?

Yes. One leader, one ExecutiveOS. You can add leaders later.
