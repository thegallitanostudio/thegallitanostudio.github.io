Questions by topic

Working with me

Who is this for?

Executives who already have the institutional track record: C-suite officers, SVPs and EVPs, managing partners, and board directors. You’ve done the work. Now own the narrative.

Is this a ghostwriting service?

No. Ghostwriting produces generic, polished noise. This is strategic positioning. We extract your actual philosophy, operational wins, and leadership convictions. You review and own every single word.

How much of my time does it take?

Known For Executive: a monthly strategic session, plus one 30-minute prep session a month (two in months with a board, earnings or investor date), and up to four short reviews between sessions. Known For First Look: two weeks, with a tailored intake, a 60-minute extraction session, the build, a 30-minute readout and 15-minute final calibration sessions. No book. No workbook.

What if my quarter gets busy?

If a board interview moves up by ten days, we accelerate. If an enterprise crisis hits, we pause. Your momentum is never lost.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Reputation is currency. Every strategic advisory engagement is strictly guarded. Your narrative belongs to you, never my portfolio.

  • First Look is for leaders paying personally. See For companies.

ExecutiveOS

What is ExecutiveOS?

One leader’s AI, built for them inside the tools your company already approves. It knows their story, their voice and their work. They set the direction. It does the first pass.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Copilot, Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT. Whichever your company has approved. There is no new software to buy from me.

Can we start with one leader?

Yes. One leader, one ExecutiveOS. You can add leaders later.
